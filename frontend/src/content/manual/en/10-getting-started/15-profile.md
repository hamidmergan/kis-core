# Language level

![The language level page with switch and level](/manual/{lang}/profile.png)

Reachable through the gear at the top right. The page only exists when the platform offers the
language trainer.

- **Switch**: on or off, applies in every module
- **Level**: the grade from the language test with its date
- **Reset level**: clears the grade, then Take the language test appears
- **Take the language test**: shows while the switch is on and no level is stored yet

Off keeps the level stored. Whoever switches back on has it back at once.
