# Profile

![The profile with the language trainer block](/manual/{lang}/profile.png)

Reachable through the gear at the top right. Name, username, email and role sit at the top.

## Language trainer

The block only appears when the platform offers the language trainer.

- **Switch**: on or off, applies in every module
- **Level**: the grade from the language test with its date
- **Reset the level and take the test again**: clears the grade and starts the test
- **Take the language test**: shows while the switch is on and no level is stored yet

Off keeps the level stored. Whoever switches back on has it back at once.
