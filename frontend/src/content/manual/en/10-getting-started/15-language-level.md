# Language level

![The language level page with level and ladder](/manual/{lang}/language-level.png)

Reachable through the gear at the top right, for every role holding the permission Own language
level and language test. The language test can be started here at any time, even when you dismissed
the invitation on the start page.

- **Level**: the grade from A1 to C2 with its name from the Common European Framework and one
  sentence on what it means. Below it says whether it comes from the language test or was chosen
  by hand, with the date
- **Ladder**: all six grades in a row, filled up to your own
- **Change level**: opens the list of grades with descriptions. The chosen grade shows at the top
  right away as a preview, it is stored only with Apply. From there you can also retake the
  language test
- **Without a level**: two ways, take the language test or set the level yourself. Only with a
  level does the language trainer appear in the modules

The level applies in every module. It is a placement for the exercises, not an exam result.
