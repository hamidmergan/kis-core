# Signing in

![The sign-in form](/manual/{lang}/sign-in.png)

Enter user name and password, click Sign in.

## Where you get an account

**Your university credentials do not work here.** KIS has its own user management.

You need an **invitation link** from the person running the course. Opening it lets you set your
password, the user name is told to you. The link works once.

## When it does not work

- **Rejected:** check capital and small letters
- **"Account not released yet":** your account exists but is not released. The page says whom to
  contact
- **Forgotten password:** no reset button, contact the person who invited you
