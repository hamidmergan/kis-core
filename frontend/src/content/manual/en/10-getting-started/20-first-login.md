# After signing in

![The start page on a first visit, here as students see it](/manual/{lang}/first-login.png)

## Welcome

On the very first login a welcome dialog opens with a text from the administration. It only
appears if the administration has switched it on and your role carries the permission See the
welcome dialog on first login. This is remembered in the browser, in another browser it comes
once more.

## The start page

The start page is the tile overview with three groups: Learning assistant, Info assistant and
Services with one tile per released service. Each group says whom it is meant for,
Teachers or Students. Whoever has only one place to go, for example students without services,
lands there directly, in the workspace list. What the services do is in the Services chapter.

## Cards with offers

Above the workspace list sit up to three cards, the evaluation card also on the tile overview.
Which you see depends on your permissions and on what the administration has switched on. Once
answered, a card is gone, all three offers stay reachable through the gear icon in the top right.

### Tour through the demo workspace

- **Requirement**: The administration has marked a demo workspace for the tour, otherwise there is
  neither a card nor a menu entry
- **Start** enrols you in the demo workspaces and begins the tour, afterwards they stay in your
  workspace list
- **Stops**: Anyone allowed to create workspaces also gets the stops about the teaching buttons
- **Breaking off**: Broken off halfway the card stays, only the last stop or **Hide** takes it
  away. Your account remembers this, not the browser

### Language trainer

- **Requirement**: the permission Own language level and language test, which no system role
  holds, only a custom role. The administration also has to switch the language trainer on for
  the platform
- **Start the language test** switches the trainer on and leads straight into the test, the result
  becomes your language level
- **Level**: decides how the learning assistant in a workspace explains and how flashcards, quizzes and
  exercises are worded
- **Hide** only records the answer, you can take the test later under Language level in the gear
  menu

### Evaluation

- **When**: only while the administration has an evaluation switched on, and only after you have
  tried something, such as a question in the chat, a service or the tour
- **Not now** is remembered by the browser until the administration starts a new round. After
  submitting, the card does not come back
- More in the article FAQ and feedback

Nothing is lost for good without the cards. What the language trainer does and what a level unlocks
is in the Language trainer chapter.
