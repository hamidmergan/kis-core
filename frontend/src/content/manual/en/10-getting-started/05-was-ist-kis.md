# What KIS is

KIS is a pilot project at TH Lübeck, an AI platform for teaching and study. It
covers two separate areas: standalone tools and modules with an AI study
assistant.

## The tools

Seven tools for individual tasks, independent of modules: upload a file,
download a result.

- **Translation** translates documents into another language and keeps the layout
- **Text to speech** and **Speech to text** convert between audio and text
- **Summary** shortens long content
- **Image generation** creates and edits images
- **Screencast** turns slides into a narrated video

## The modules

A module bundles the materials on a topic, for example a lecture. The AI study
assistant answers questions about it with evidence from exactly these
materials. Depending on the module, this comes with a subject trainer for
flashcards and quizzes on the content, and a language trainer for students
still learning the course language.

## Who it is for

Teaching staff create modules and invite people through a link. The tools are
open to every account, which ones depends on the university's administration.
