# What KIS is

KIS stands for KInternationalisierung von Studiengängen and is a pilot project at TH Lübeck, an AI
platform for teaching and study. It covers two separate areas: the AI assistant Francis in
workspaces and standalone services.

## The AI assistant Francis

A workspace bundles the materials on a topic, for example a lecture. The AI assistant Francis
answers questions about it from exactly these materials only, with a citation on every sentence.
On top come the tools in the workspace's studio, such as flashcards and quizzes on the content,
and, where it is switched on, a language trainer for German as a foreign language.

## The services

Ten services for individual tasks, independent of workspaces: upload material, download a result.

- **Translation** translates texts and documents, currently between German and English, layout and
  formatting stay intact
- **Text to audio** reads texts and documents aloud with natural sounding voices
- **Audio to text** transcribes audio and video, also as subtitles with timestamps
- **Summary** merges several materials into one text, as a short briefing, study guide or timeline
- **Screencast** turns PowerPoint slides into a narrated video, optionally in another language
- **Podcast** turns material into a conversation to listen to, you check the script and have it voiced
- **Presentation** creates slides from material in the university's design
- **Image generation** creates images, adapts existing ones or describes them
- **Interpreter** translates a conversation between two languages as it goes, spoken or typed
- **Glossary** keeps your own term lists for consistent terminology in translations

## Who it is for

Teaching staff create workspaces and invite students through a link. The services are meant for
teaching staff, on the start page the group carries the tag Teachers. Which services an account
sees depends on the permissions of its role and on what the university's administration has
released.
