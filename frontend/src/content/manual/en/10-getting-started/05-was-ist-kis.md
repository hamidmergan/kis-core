# What KIS is

KIS stands for KInternationalisierung von Studiengängen and is a pilot project
at TH Lübeck, an AI platform for teaching and study. It covers two separate
areas: standalone services and workspaces with an AI study assistant.

## The services

Seven services for individual tasks, independent of workspaces: upload a file,
download a result.

- **Translation** translates documents into another language and keeps the layout
- **Text to audio** and **Audio to text** convert between sound and writing
- **Summary** shortens long content
- **Image generation** creates and edits images
- **Screencast** turns slides into a narrated video

## The workspaces

A workspace bundles the materials on a topic, for example a lecture. The AI study
assistant answers questions about it with evidence from exactly these
materials. Depending on the workspace, this comes with a subject trainer for
flashcards and quizzes on the content, and a language trainer for students
still learning the course language.

## Who it is for

Teaching staff create workspaces and invite people through a link. The services are
open to every account, which ones depends on the university's administration.
