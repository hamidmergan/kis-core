# Settings inside a module

![The settings inside a module](/manual/{lang}/module-settings.png)

The gear inside an opened module applies to that module only and comes in two parts.

**Personal** applies to you alone, others in the module see nothing of it:

- which mascot accompanies you in the chat
- whether the studio is shown
- whether you use the language trainer, along with your level and a button to delete it
- which language the selection bar in the PDF translates marked text into

**Module** applies to everybody and can only be changed by the administration of the module:

- whether the language trainer is released in the module at all
- whether transcripts of audio and video are translated, and into which language
