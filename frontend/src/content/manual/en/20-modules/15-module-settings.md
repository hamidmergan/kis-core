# Settings inside a workspace

![The settings inside a workspace](/manual/{lang}/module-settings.png)

The gear inside an opened workspace applies to you alone, others in the workspace see nothing of
it. KIS remembers the choice per person, so it holds in another browser too.

- **Studio**: whether the panel on the right is shown
- **Student view**: owners and tutors only, see below
- **Translation language in the selection bar**: which language selected text in the PDF is
  translated into
- At the bottom the link **Report a bug or share an idea**

## The student view

The switch shows you the workspace the way students see it. A banner above the panels says the
preview is on, and every control of the workspace management is gone. You do not see the
students' notes or chat history, those belong to each person alone.

In the demo workspace it is the other way round: whoever is only enrolled there but may create
workspaces themselves gets a **Teacher view**. It is on from the start, the controls of the
workspace management have a dashed outline and do nothing.
