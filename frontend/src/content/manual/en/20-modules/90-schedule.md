# Schedule

![The Schedule tab in the studio](/manual/{lang}/schedule-tab.png)

Dates from the materials: deadlines, exams, lectures. The schedule is personal. What you extract
or enter is seen by you alone, students build their own.

## Where they come from

Not on their own. The button **Extract dates** reads the ticked materials through and takes what
has to be attended or met in the course: exams, submissions, sessions, office hours, deadlines
and periods like the lecture period. Dates without course relevance such as revision dates or
citations stay out.

- A material is read only once. The line above the button says how many of the ticked materials
  are already processed
- When new materials arrive, press the button again
- If the source gives no year, KIS takes the year that puts the date closest to today. Such
  dates carry a small question mark that explains it
- A deleted material takes its dates along

## Citations on a date

Below every date sit citations like in the chat. One click jumps to the spot the date came from.

## Without a date and across several days

Dates without a fixed day sit under **By arrangement**. Time spans stand as one date and only
turn grey after the end.

## Editing

The pencil in the top right switches to edit mode. Then there is **New entry**, a pencil and
delete on every date, and **Delete all**.

- An entry has a title, date and time, plus **Runs until** and **Ends at** for a span
- **Month only, no day** for dates whose day is not fixed yet
- **Optional** marks what is voluntary
- **Delete all** empties the schedule and makes every material readable again

The tick in front of a date marks it as done.

## In the calendar

The calendar button at the top of the schedule opens the calendar in a new tab. There the dates
of all workspaces stand together. Only there can a date be hidden, and only this browser
remembers it. More in the article Calendar.
