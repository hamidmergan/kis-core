# Schedule

![The Schedule tab in the studio](/manual/{lang}/schedule-tab.png)

Dates from the materials: deadlines, exams, lectures.

## Where they come from

Not on their own. The button **Extract dates** reads the ticked materials through. When new
materials arrive, press it again. Your own dates you enter by hand.

## Citations on a date

Below every date sit citations like in the chat. One click jumps to the spot the date came from.

## Without a date and across several days

Dates without a fixed day sit under **By arrangement**. Time spans stand as one date and only
turn grey after the end.

## In the calendar

Every date can be shown or hidden. The calendar on top then shows all modules together.
