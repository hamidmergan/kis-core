# Statistics inside the module

The Statistics tab shows your own state: how many sets you worked through, how much of it was
right, where it gets stuck.

![The Statistics tab in the studio](/manual/{lang}/statistics-tab.png)

## Your own state only

Even for teachers this tab shows only their own numbers, not the participants'. An overview of
the whole group is under Statistics → My modules in the administration area.

## What it is not

Not a grade and not an assessment. The numbers say what was used, not how good somebody is.
Whoever runs an exercise three times sees different numbers than somebody who solves it once
correctly.
