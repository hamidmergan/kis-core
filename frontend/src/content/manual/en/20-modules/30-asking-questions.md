# Asking questions

![The chat with a question and an answer](/manual/{lang}/chat.png)

Type the question, send it. Below the field you see how many materials are active. While the
chat is empty, three suggestions wait for you.

## From the materials only

If something is not in the materials, the chat says so instead of making it up. A question about
grammar therefore stays unanswered as long as no grammar material is uploaded.

## Follow-up questions

"Explain the second point in more detail" works, the chat resolves the reference.

## With an answer

- **Copy** to the clipboard
- **Save as note** files it in the studio
- **Show all citations** reveals where every sentence came from

## The history

The history is kept per module and person. Before a new, independent question it pays to
**clear the chat log** through the three dots, otherwise the chat keeps the old question in mind.

## The mascot

Hilde or Francis, your choice in the settings of the module. It changes nothing about the
content.

![Hilde in her five moods](/manual/de/mascot-states.png)

The picture sits above the newest answer and shows what the chat is up to. From left to right:

- Idle, for an answer without citations
- Thinking, while the answer is being written
- Found it, when the finished answer carries citations
- Confused, when the materials say nothing about the question
- Waving, while the chat is still empty

Only the newest answer carries the picture, older ones stand without it.
