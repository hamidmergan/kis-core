# Creating a workspace

![Creating a new workspace](/manual/{lang}/create-module.png)

On the workspace list through **+ Workspace**. The title may be 50 characters long, the
description 500 characters.

- The first enrollment link is created right along with it. The date in the window is its
  validity, empty means unlimited
- A new workspace starts out inactive. Only the switch in the list lets participants in

## Getting participants in

Nobody is entered by hand. You pass on an **enrollment link**, and whoever opens it enrolls
themselves.

Every link is shown twice, as the full address and as a short code next to it, both copied with one
click. The code is for the ways without a click, on a slide, on the board, read out in class. With
it people get in through **Join** on the workspace list.

- **At most 10 links** per workspace, each with its own validity. For a new one an old one has to go
- **Separate chat histories**: whoever enrolls through different links only sees their own group
  in the chat. That way two courses can share the same material and stay apart
- An expired link is shown struck through in the list
- **Deleting** a link makes it invalid, whoever enrolled through it stays in
- **100 members** per workspace, after that the row in the list carries the mark Full

## Sharing from inside the workspace

![Sharing inside an opened workspace](/manual/{lang}/share-module.png)

Inside an opened workspace the **Share** button sits next to the gear. It puts the enrollment links
and the participant list into one window, together with the active switch, so a link never leads
nowhere. Owners and tutors see it, nobody else.

Under participants you make single people **tutors**. Tutors may do everything except deleting
and appointing further tutors. Only the owner can appoint them.

## The menu of the workspace row

- **Participants**: list, appointing tutors
- **Enrollment links**: create and withdraw access
- **Edit**: title and description. For a demo workspace the tick **Workspace for the guided
  tour** sits here too. The tour then starts in this workspace, every other demo workspace loses
  the tick
- **Delete**: owners only, final, with everything attached. The prompt says how many
  participants lose their chat messages and notes with it
