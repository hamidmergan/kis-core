# Checking citations

![Unfolded citations behind a sentence](/manual/{lang}/citations.png)

The round marks behind the sentences are toggles. On a computer the citations already unfold when
the mouse passes over them, a click keeps them open. Each citation names material and spot, at
most three per sentence.

Clicking a citation opens the material on the left, jumps to the page or time mark and shades the
passage light blue. The clicked citation keeps its outline until you clear the highlight in the
viewer.

![After the click: the material jumps to the spot](/manual/{lang}/citation-jump.png)

## All citations at once

Below the answer, the left side says **n sources**, a click lists them. A source deleted from the
workspace since is greyed out and opens nothing any more. Next to it on the right sits a round
button. On a computer it appears as soon as you move the mouse over the answer, on a phone it is
always there.

![The button for all citations below the answer](/manual/{lang}/citations-toggle.png)

One click unfolds the citations of every sentence at once, the next folds them back. While they
are open, the button stays visible.

## When there is no citation

Headings and linking sentences do not get one. A broad overview has fewer than a pointed
question.

## Why this counts

The answer comes from an AI. The citation is the built-in way to hold it against the original.
