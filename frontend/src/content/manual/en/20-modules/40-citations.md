# Checking citations

![Unfolded citations behind a sentence](/manual/{lang}/citations.png)

The round marks behind the sentences are toggles. One click unfolds the citations, each naming
material and spot.

Clicking a citation opens the material on the left, jumps to the page or time mark and
highlights the passage.

![After the click: the material jumps to the spot](/manual/{lang}/citation-jump.png)

**Show all citations** reveals them for the whole answer at once.

## When there is no citation

Headings and linking sentences do not get one. A broad overview has fewer than a pointed
question.

## Why this counts

The answer comes from an AI. The citation is the built-in way to hold it against the original.
