# Notes and glossary

Two tabs in the studio collect what you want to keep. Both belong to you alone, nobody else in the
workspace sees them.

## Notes

![The Notes tab in the studio](/manual/{lang}/notes-tab.png)

Type at the top and file it with the plus. A note also comes from a chat answer through **Save
as note**, from a text selection in the PDF through **To note** or from a passage of the
transcript.

- The title is created from the content automatically, you can change it
- Citations in the note stay jump marks, a click leads to the spot in the material
- At most 100 notes per person and workspace

## Glossary

![The Glossary tab in the studio](/manual/{lang}/glossary-tab.png)

Terms only arrive through the **selection bar in the PDF**: select text, **Add to glossary**.
Clicking an entry unfolds it, with base form, context, AI explanation and page number.

- **Term, context sentence and page number** are taken word for word from the selected passage. A
  sentence the AI rephrases is dropped, the entry then stays without a context sentence
- **Translation and explanation** are written by the AI itself. When you select a single word or a
  short phrase, it also reads the surrounding text on the page, so the explanation fits that use and
  not the general dictionary meaning
- Only the context sentence is checked, not the explanation. It is not a quote from the document,
  even though the entry points to a page
- How simple the explanation gets follows your language level from the language trainer. Without
  the language trainer or without a set level the AI writes at B1
- At most 150 terms per person and workspace

Sort by newest or A to Z. The bin icon deletes one entry, **Delete all terms** in the menu empties
the glossary.

Flashcards and vocab cards can be generated from the glossary. Collect properly first, then
practise.

## Do not mix them up

This glossary belongs to the workspace. The **glossary service** on the start page is something else:
term lists for translations.
