# Notes and glossary

Two tabs in the studio collect what you want to keep.

## Notes

![The Notes tab in the studio](/manual/{lang}/notes-tab.png)

Type at the top and file it with the plus. A note also comes from a chat answer through **Save
as note** or from a text selection in the PDF through **To note**.

The title is created from the content automatically, you can change it.

## Glossary

![The Glossary tab in the studio](/manual/{lang}/glossary-tab.png)

Terms only arrive through the **selection bar in the PDF**: select text, **To glossary**. The AI
pulls the technical words out of it, with translation, definition and page number.

Sort by newest or A to Z. Clicking an entry unfolds the definition.

The glossary is the basis for vocabulary cards and vocabulary exercises. Collect properly first,
then practise.

## Do not mix them up

This glossary belongs to the module. The **glossary tool** in the left bar is something else:
term lists for translations.
