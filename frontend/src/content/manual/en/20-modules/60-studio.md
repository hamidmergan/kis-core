# The studio

The studio sits on the right inside the workspace. It is the workbench next to the chat and has
five tabs.

![The studio with the tiles of the tools](/manual/{lang}/module-view.png)

- **Tools** holds the tiles that exercises and overviews are built from
- **Glossary** collects the technical terms of the workspace
- **Notes** keeps what you want to hold on to
- **Stats** shows your own state, for teachers too only their own. The numbers of the whole
  group sit on the workspace list
- **Schedule** lists the dates that sit inside the materials

## The tiles

The tools come in two groups:

- **Technical language trainer**: Flashcards, Quiz, Data table, Mind map
- **Language trainer**: Vocab cards, Vocabulary exercises, Grammar exercises. The group only
  appears when the language trainer is switched on

Every tile works with the ticked materials. When creating, an instruction can be given that
narrows the topic.

**Recent activity** sits below the tiles. Everything created lately lands there and one click
takes you back to it.

## Hiding it

Whoever does not need the studio hides it in the settings of the workspace for themselves. Chat and
materials then get the whole space.
