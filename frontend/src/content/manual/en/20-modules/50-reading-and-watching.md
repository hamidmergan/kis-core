# Reading and watching

![A material in the viewer](/manual/{lang}/viewer.png)

Clicking a material opens it on the left, in place of the list. The arrow in the top left takes
you back to the list.

## The selection bar in the PDF

![The selection bar below a highlighted passage](/manual/{lang}/selection-bar.png)

Select text and seven buttons appear:

- **Copy**
- **Highlight** colours the selection in the PDF
- **Add to glossary**
- **To note**
- **Ask a question** hands the selection to the chat
- **Translate** from one word up
- **Explain** from three words up, puts it in simpler terms

You set the target language for translate in the gear of the workspace.

## Audio and video

![A video with its transcript](/manual/{lang}/video.png)

The transcript below the player runs along, every passage with a time mark. A click on a passage
plays from there. Above the list you choose: original language, translation, both or nothing.

- As soon as you scroll yourself, the transcript stops following along. The arrow next to the
  word Transcript switches following off and on again and jumps to the current spot
- The note icon on a passage, **Save as note**, files it with its time mark in the studio. From
  the note you jump back into the video

As long as there is no translation, an entry with a plus hangs at the bottom of that same list. It
opens a window for picking the language and is visible only to owners and tutors. The
translation runs in the background and can take a few minutes, a spinner turns next to the list
meanwhile and the entry names the chosen language. There is exactly one translation per material,
a new language replaces the previous one.

## Correcting the transcript

Speech recognition mishears things. Owners and tutors correct the wording right on the passage,
without a new upload.

- Start in the recording's menu in the list of materials through **Edit transcript**. The video
  opens and every passage shows a pencil
- Only change single words. Whoever rewrites a whole line detaches the text from what is said at
  that spot, and jumps from the chat land next to it
- A line may be 1,000 characters long and must not be left empty, shorten rather than delete
- The correction also lands in the version the chat searches. It quotes the right wording
  afterwards

## The highlight from a citation

When you arrive here through a citation from the chat, the passage lies in front of you shaded
light blue. Two round buttons then sit in the bottom right corner.

![The two buttons next to a highlight](/manual/{lang}/highlight-controls.png)

- The arrow with the page number takes you back to the highlight. It only appears once you have
  paged away, and it points up or down, depending on where the passage sits
- The X clears the highlight, and the outline on the citation in the chat goes with it

For audio and video the two sit next to the transcript. There the time mark stands in place of
the page number, and a click plays on from that spot.
