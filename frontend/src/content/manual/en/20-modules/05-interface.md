# Workspace list

![The workspace list with your own workspaces](/manual/{lang}/module-list.png)

Reached through the **Learning assistant** tile on the start page. Anyone with nothing but the
assistant released lands here straight after signing in.

## The header

- **Services** only appears when at least one service is released, and so does **Jobs**
- **Calendar** only comes together with the learning assistant
- **Gear**: language, light or dark, and depending on your rights language level, interfaces,
  tour through the demo workspace, evaluation and documentation. The entry for the
  administration is called Admin, Administration or Statistics, depending on what you may do.
  More in the article Settings
- On a phone all of it sits in one menu

## What the list holds

Your own and tutored workspaces sit under **My Workspaces**, the ones you joined as a participant
under **Enrolled**. More than 20 workspaces spread over several pages.

- **Tutor** behind the name: you tutor the workspace, it belongs to somebody else
- **Demo** marks the demo workspace. It can be left through the menu of its row, a normal
  workspace cannot
- **Full** appears from 100 members on, nobody can enroll after that
- A long description is cut short and unfolds on a click
- The **chart icon** opens the numbers of the whole workspace, see Statistics inside the
  workspace

## Getting into a workspace

![Joining with a link or a code](/manual/{lang}/join-module.png)

Next to **+ Workspace** sits **Join**. Paste in what you were given, the whole address or just the
code from it.

- Upper or lower case makes no difference, and a space in the middle of the code does no harm
- An unknown code says so inside the window, the page does not change
- After that comes the enrollment card with the workspace name and the teacher, only the button
  on it enrolls you
- If you are not in any workspace yet and may not create one, the same field sits right in the
  empty list. If there is an open demo workspace, the card **No workspace yet** with **Add demo
  workspace** sits next to it

## Active and inactive

The switch behind each workspace locks it for participants. Nothing is deleted, one click releases
it again.

- Enrolled people still see a locked workspace in their list, greyed out and marked **Not
  open**. Their enrollment stays in place
- An enrollment link works while the workspace is locked. The person is enrolled and gets in as
  soon as you open it
