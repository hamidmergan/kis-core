# Statistics

Under Statistics sit numbers to read, five tabs, each bound to its own right. Nothing is set here.

- **Users**: who is online right now, the daily course, online per day, new accounts per month,
  roles, plus a view of a single person.
- **Services**: jobs per service, load by weekday and hour.
- **AI assistant**: workspaces, exercises, chat, levels, materials, tools, also per single
  workspace.
- **Models**: calls and tokens per model and provider. So far only chat is recorded.
- **Evaluation**: participations, numbered rather than named.
