# Limits

Under Limits sit every upper bound for size, volume, and context, in two tabs.

## Material

File size, page count, and recording length, plus how many materials a workspace may carry and
how many characters a single document may have. Also how many jobs a person may run at once
(services with a voice count apart from the rest) and how many finished jobs are kept before the
oldest one disappears automatically. There is no fixed deadline, only this number.

## Context

How much text the AI assistant Francis and the tools may give the model per request, separately
for overview questions, specific questions, and the tools. Too small a value makes jobs fail at
the model's window, and only lowering it helps.
