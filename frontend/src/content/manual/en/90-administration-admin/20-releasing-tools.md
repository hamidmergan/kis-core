# Releasing and limiting services

Under Services you control which of the ten services appear on the start page, and see every job.

![Releasing the services](/manual/{lang}/admin-tool-release.png)

## Release

Each service switches on or off on its own. Off means the service disappears from the start page
for everyone, and the address no longer gets anyone in either. A person's role additionally
decides whether they see a released service.

## Jobs

The list of every job of every person, with user, service, file, status, and time. This is where
the search starts when someone reports that something is stuck. Searchable by user, service, or
file, narrowable by period, 50 or 100 entries per page. A waiting or running job can be cancelled,
which deletes it at the same time. Jobs is the page that shows first on opening the administration.

## Not here

Upper limits for file sizes, page counts, and running jobs sit under Limits, how often a person
may use a service sits under Users at the quotas.
