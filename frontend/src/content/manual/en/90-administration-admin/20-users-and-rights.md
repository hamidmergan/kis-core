# Users and rights

Under Users you decide who gets in and who may do what.


## Who gets in

- **Pending**: requests waiting for release
- **Whitelist**: who gets in without a release
- **Invitations**: access by link, as long as there is no central sign-on

## Who may do what

- **"All users" tab**: search, set the role, open quotas
- **"Roles" tab**: the system roles are fixed, your own roles are put together freely
- **"Quotas" tab**: how often somebody may use a service

## Roles and workspaces

The role governs what somebody may do on the platform. Who owns a workspace and who is a tutor in
it is governed by the workspace itself. The two are independent of each other.

## Deleting

Deleting a person also removes everything attached to them. This is final.
