# Users and rights

Under Users you decide who gets in and who may do what.

## Who gets in

- **Pending**: requests waiting for release.
- **Whitelist**: a switch plus a list of addresses that get in without release.
- **Invites** and **access codes**: a separate article, see Access codes and invites.

## All users

Search, role, status, the demo checkbox (exactly one account whose workspaces appear for
everyone on the start page), impersonation, and deleting the account. Deleting removes everything
hanging off the person, workspaces, sources, chat messages, results, enrolments, and cannot be
undone. The main admin from the server settings can neither be changed nor deleted, nor can your
own account.

## Roles

A matrix, roles as columns, functions as rows. One click saves immediately. The system roles
Admin, Staff, and User are fixed, you put custom roles together freely. Deleting a role drops its
people back to User.

Two rights sit only in custom roles, no system role carries them: Own language level and language
test (the language trainer hangs off this) and Create own interfaces to voice services (Staff has
this by default).

## Quotas

How often a person may use a service, reachable through Details in All users. The default limits
are fixed in code, only the individual override changes here.

## Impersonations

A log of who was in whose account when. Not possible for yourself, the main admin, other admin
accounts, and roles that may manage rights themselves.

## Roles and workspaces

The role governs what someone may do on the platform. Who owns a workspace and who is a tutor in
it is governed by the workspace itself.
