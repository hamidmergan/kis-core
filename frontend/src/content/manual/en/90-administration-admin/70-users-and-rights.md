# Users and rights

Under Users you decide who gets in and who may do what.

## Who gets in

- **Pending**: requests waiting for release.
- **Whitelist**: a switch plus a list of addresses that get in without release.
- **Invites** and **access codes**: a separate article, see Access codes and invites.

## All users

Search by name, username, or email, plus role, status active/inactive, the demo checkbox (exactly
one account whose workspaces appear for everyone on the start page), impersonation, and deleting
the account. The main admin from the server settings can neither be changed nor deleted, nor can
your own account.

Deleting removes everything hanging off the person: workspaces with every file, sources including
transcripts, chat messages, results from the services, enrolments in other people's workspaces.
This cannot be undone.

## Roles

A matrix, roles as columns, functions as rows, with a Use and a Manage chapter each. One click
saves immediately. The system roles Admin, Staff, and User are fixed, you put custom roles
together freely, a new one starts empty. Deleting a role drops its people back to User.

Two rights sit only in custom roles, no system role carries them by default: Own language level
and language test (the language trainer hangs off this) and Create own interfaces to voice
services (Staff has this by default).

## Quotas

No entry of its own, reachable through Details in the Quotas column of All users. Per service:
used, limit (default), override (-1 unlimited, empty follows the default), and reset. The default
limits themselves, 100 jobs per 4 hours by default except presentation without a cap, are fixed in
code and cannot be changed here.

## Impersonations

A log of who was in whose account when, and how it ended. The button for it sits in All users and
is missing for yourself, inactive accounts, the main admin, other admin accounts, and roles that
may manage rights themselves.

## Roles and workspaces

The role governs what someone may do on the platform. Who owns a workspace and who is a tutor in
it is governed by the workspace itself. The two are independent of each other.
