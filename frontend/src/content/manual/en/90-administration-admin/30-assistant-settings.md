# Setting up the learning assistant

Under Learning assistant you decide how material is read in and how the language trainer works.

## Materials

- **OCR for scans and image pages**: on means more thorough and slower, off means faster, such
  pages then stay empty in the search.
- **Language of the tools**: which language flashcards, quiz, data table, and mind map are
  written in. The language trainer always stays in German.
- **Reindex all materials**: needed after switching the embedding model, needs maintenance mode
  beforehand.
- **Clear the stocks**: throws away platform-wide prepared but unused exercises and schedule
  extractions. Finished sets and entered dates stay.

## Language level

**Assessment**: the switch for the language trainer on the whole platform, plus the language
test's question catalogue, either written by hand or once by the AI. Who actually sees the
trainer additionally needs a right of its own in the rights matrix, which no system role carries
by default.

**AI behaviour**: the description of levels A1 to C2, the basis for both the assessment and the
adapted answers in chat, plus the prompt texts under Advanced.
