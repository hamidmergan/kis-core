# Quiz

![A quiz question](/manual/{lang}/quiz-question.png)

Questions to tick. A quiz mixes three kinds at about a third each:

- comprehension questions with four answers
- gapped sentences with four answers
- statements that are true or false

With an instruction the split is only the default. "Only true or false questions", for example,
gives a quiz without the other two kinds.

A citation sits below the question, just like in the chat. **Hint** helps along without giving
the solution away. After answering, Learn more opens the question in the chat.
