# Flashcards

![A flashcard](/manual/{lang}/flashcard.png)

Cards with a question and an answer from the materials. On the front, Hint helps along without
giving the solution away. After revealing the answer you say whether you knew it, and Learn more
opens the question in the chat.

## Going through

- **Changing a rating**: going back shows the card on its answer side, just click the other
  button
- **Back and Next always work**: a card can be skipped and rated later
- **Open cards**: if cards are still unrated at the end, KIS asks. View open jumps to the first of
  them, Finish anyway counts only the rated ones

At the end it says how many you knew, plus the number of wrong and open ones.

## Do not mix them up with vocab cards

Flashcards ask about the **content**. The **vocab cards** in the language trainer explain a
technical word linguistically.
