# Info assistant

The info assistant answers questions about the university: applying, enrolment, deadlines, advisory services, buildings and campus life. It answers only from the university's web pages, from uploaded documents and from the place list of the campus map. Every answer shows its sources.

## Questions and sources

Below each answer stand up to five source cards with the page title, the address and the date the page was last fetched. A click opens the original page in a new tab. In Chrome and Edge the browser jumps to the matching passage and highlights it, other browsers open the page as usual.

When an answer names a building or a facility, a place card appears. It opens the campus map at that spot in a window, a button leads to a new tab.

When something is not in the sources, the assistant says so and names the responsible office if the sources know it.

## History

The history sits on the left, grouped into Today, Last 7 days and Older. Every chat can be renamed and deleted. On the phone the menu at the top left opens the history.

Follow-up questions refer to the previous round of the same chat. A new topic is best started with **New chat**.

## Limits

A question holds at most 2,000 characters, a chat at most 50 questions, after that a new chat begins. Each person keeps at most 100 chats, the oldest drops out. Chats without activity are deleted after a period set by the administration, the note stands below the history.

## For administrators

Under **Administration → Info assistant** you find the sitemap of the university site, patterns for addresses to include or exclude, the address of the campus map, the place list and the retention of chats. The refresh of the pages runs every night and can be started there right away. Changed pages are read again, vanished ones removed. Documents that are not on the website can be uploaded there.
