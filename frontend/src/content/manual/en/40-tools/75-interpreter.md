# Interpreter

Two languages, one conversation. Each side has its own field in its language, the translation
runs alongside. Everything stays in the history.

![The interpreter with two languages](/manual/{lang}/tool-interpreter.png)

## How a conversation runs

- **Speak** starts the recording, **Done** ends it, or the other side presses Speak and takes
  over. While you are still speaking, a live translation runs alongside, after Done the sentence
  waits for the complete recognition.
- **Typing** works the same way, Enter or Translate sends the sentence.
- **Correcting**: if the AI gets a word wrong, change the sentence right in the field.
- **Swap sides** turns the languages around. Currently there are German and English.
- **Names**: Person 1 and Person 2 can be renamed, up to 80 characters.

## Limits

- **One recording** ends by itself after 5 minutes, from 4:30 minutes the card says that it is
  nearly over.
- **One typed sentence** may have 2,000 characters.
- **One conversation** ends at 20,000 characters. Only what was said and typed counts, not the
  translation. From 18,000 characters the page warns. A full conversation stays readable, New
  conversation carries on right away.
- **Past conversations**: every conversation you start is saved, the last 100 per account. When
  a new one starts, the one untouched longest drops out.

## Settings

Behind the gear you choose the AI provider for speech recognition and the one for translation,
plus up to 3 glossaries. Audio and text only go to the providers chosen here, the browser's own
speech recognition is not used.

## Transcript and past conversations

- **Transcript** shows both languages in the order of the conversation and downloads them as a
  text file, with time and name in front of every sentence.
- **Past conversations** opens a saved conversation to read it again or to continue, or deletes
  it.

The interpreter creates no jobs and has no quota.
