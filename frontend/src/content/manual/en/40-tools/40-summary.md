# Report

Brings several sources together into one text, as a short briefing, study guide or timeline.

![The report service](/manual/{lang}/tool-summary.png)

## Sources

On the Files tab you upload up to 10 files, as PDF, Word, PowerPoint, text, Markdown or
subtitles. On the Workspace tab you take sources from one of your workspaces instead. The limits
for pages, file size and the 500,000 characters for everything together are in the overview of
the services, the counter on the page shows them while you pick.

If there are several AI providers, you choose provider and model. A model with a window below
50,000 tokens is refused by the service, every other one fits: long texts are split into parts
for the planning.

## Template and instruction

- **Short briefing**, **Study guide** or **Timeline**: the template decides what is read out of
  the sources first and how the text is organised. The number of sections depends on how much
  the sources contain.
- **Instruction, optional**: narrows down to a topic or shapes the text, such as only chapter 2
  or for first-year students. How the AI sorts it is in the overview of the services.

## How the text comes about

The sources are read and worked through one by one, then the structure is planned across the
whole text, then it is written section by section. With several sources the plan makes sure that
no file falls by the wayside, unless the instruction names a topic. A law of a good 200 pages
takes a few minutes, the job keeps running in the background.

## The result

- **Download MD** gives the text as Markdown, with title, date and the AI notice above it.
- **Download DOCX** gives the same report as a Word file. It only gets a table of contents from
  5 headings on.

Where your material names the part a statement rests on, such as a section or an article, that
reference stands behind the statement. The text never names page numbers.

## What to expect

A report is a shortening and inevitably leaves something out. It does not replace the original
when accuracy matters.

## The difference to the chat inside a workspace

The service delivers a text to download. The chat inside a workspace works across all ticked
materials and backs every statement with the spot it came from.
