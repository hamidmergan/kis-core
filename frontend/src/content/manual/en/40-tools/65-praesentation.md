# Presentation

From files or the sources of a workspace comes a PowerPoint file in the university's slide master or in a template of your own. The AI only fills the placeholders that the template's layouts provide.

## Template

The THL template is set by default. You upload a template of your own as pptx or potx, only the first slide master counts. If the template has no image fields, no images are created. Every template of your own comes with an image style in English that adapts the AI images to the design.

The preview can only show fonts that are on the server. It replaces other fonts, in PowerPoint the file looks right if the font is installed there.

## Size

How many slides there are is decided by the AI from the amount of material, at most 30. You steer this with the instruction, for example by asking for a short overview. Once you start, structure, texts and images run through without a stop in between.

## Slides

Every slide shows a preview and the speaker note. You change the slide texts themselves in PowerPoint after downloading. Bullet points that are too long are split across two slides, the font is never made smaller. Tables and charts are created as real PowerPoint objects, you can change the values there.

With new text and new image you have individual slides reworked, with or without a request. A failed image marks the slide, the file is still built.

The plus sign adds a slide at that spot. You give it a title, an instruction, or both. For sources from a workspace the material is searched for that one slide instead of reusing the excerpt from the start.

## Notes and screencast

With the speaker notes checkbox every slide gets a spoken text in the notes view. Above the slide list you can untick it again, then the notes stay out of the file. The texts themselves are kept and come back unchanged when you tick it. The finished file can then be narrated directly in the screencast.
