# Presentation

From files or the sources of a workspace comes a PowerPoint file in the university's slide master
or in a template of your own. The AI only fills the placeholders that the template's layouts
provide.

![The presentation service](/manual/{lang}/tool-praesentation.png)

## Three steps

- **Content**: up to 10 files or workspace sources, at most 500,000 characters together, the
  limits are in the overview of the services. Plus slide master, Slide language, AI provider
  with model, Name on the cover, a Content instruction and the checkbox for speaker notes. The
  cover carries the date.
- **Preview**: first the outline to rearrange, then the slides take shape. Every slide appears
  as a skeleton and fills while texts and images are written.
- **Done**: Download PowerPoint.

## Template

The THL template is set by default. You upload a template of your own as pptx up to 30 MB,
without macros, only the first slide master counts, at most 10 saved templates. If the template
has no image fields, no images are created.

A template comes with an optional image style in English. On every run the Content step shows it
as the Instruction for the images, which you can change for this run, Reset to default brings the
template's style back. Images are always created without lettering.

The preview can only show fonts that are on the server. It replaces other fonts, in PowerPoint
the file looks right if the font is installed there.

## Size and instruction

How many slides there are is decided by the AI from the amount of material, at least 3, at most
30. You steer with the instruction, a topic narrows down, a wish about the manner only shapes the
text. How the AI sorts it is in the overview of the services. If the topic does not fit the
material, the preview says so and uses all of the material.

## What ends up on the slides

Beside bullet points and images there are tables and charts as real PowerPoint objects, whose
values you change there, as well as cards, a highlight, a flow, key figures, levels and symbols.
Cards come on at most every third slide that could carry them, otherwise the whole thing reads
like a template.

An overfull slide is split in two when both halves keep enough points, at least 5 per slide and 3
per column. Otherwise the font is made smaller, down to 12 pt and not below.

## Reworking slides

- **Speaker notes** you change right in the preview.
- **New text** suggests the slide again, with or without a request. You apply the suggestion for
  a selection or the whole slide.
- **New image** creates the image with the same or a changed description. A failed image marks
  the slide, the file is still built.
- **Insert slide after** adds a slide with a title, an instruction or both, from the material of
  the run.
- **Moving** and **deleting** works in the outline as in the preview.

## Cancelling and resuming

Cancelling during the build keeps finished slides, Resume carries on later. The presentation has
no quota, only the limit for open jobs applies.

## Notes and screencast

With the speaker notes checkbox every slide gets a spoken text in the notes view. In the preview
you untick it again, then the notes stay out of the file, the texts themselves are kept. The
finished file can then be narrated directly in the screencast.

Every slide carries the notice AI generated in the bottom right.
