# Podcast

Your material becomes a conversation to listen to. You pick the content and the cast, check the
script and have it voiced. The result is an MP3 file.

![The podcast service](/manual/{lang}/tool-podcast.png)

## Five steps

1. **Content**: up to 10 files as PDF, Word, PowerPoint or text, at most 500 MB and 500,000
   characters together, or up to 10 sources from a workspace. Plus the Podcast language, German
   or English, the AI provider with model, a glossary and an instruction. With Your own script
   the script comes from you, with no AI at all.
2. **Cast**: who speaks.
3. **Script**: check and change.
4. **Voicing**: runs in the background.
5. **Done**: Download MP3, Download transcript or Edit the script.

## Cast

- **1 to 4 people**: one person gives a talk, two are host and expert in conversation, three add
  someone who asks questions, four are a round with disagreement.
- **Per person** name, role and voice. Roles are Host, Expert, Student and Critical voice. Your
  own voices are added under Interfaces in the gear menu.
- **Speed** from 0.7x to 1.2x for everyone. A different tempo later voices every line again.
- **Length**: Short at about 5 minutes, Medium at about 10 minutes, Long at about 20 minutes.
- **Style**: Explanatory, Casual or Interview.

The cast can still be swapped later. Lines with a different voice are then voiced again, all
others stay. Length and style are fixed once the script is written, single lines you shorten or
delete in the script.

## What the AI may do

Facts, figures, deadlines and conditions come only from the material. Examples, comparisons and
objections it may bring itself, so the matter becomes vivid, and the people bring their own
observations. Numbers and formulas are written out, because the text is read aloud.

Glossary terms that appear in the material are handed to the AI as a guideline. It usually keeps
to them, but there is no guarantee.

## Script

The script is written in parts, finished lines already stand below while it is being written.
Afterwards every line can be changed, rephrased with Francis, deleted or added, and the speaking
person can be switched. Voicing starts only when you are happy with it.

At most 250 lines, 100,000 characters together, 1,200 characters per line.

## Voicing

The display counts the lines, finished ones can already be listened to. Cancelling keeps what is
voiced and can be continued. After several failed attempts the job stands on Paused, Continue
voicing picks up there.

If you change a line afterwards, only that one is voiced again on the next run.

## The result

The MP3 file carries the notice in its file information that it was created with AI. The
transcript is plain text with the name in front of every line.

The podcast has a quota of 100 jobs per 4 hours and counts as a service with a voice, see the
overview of the services.
