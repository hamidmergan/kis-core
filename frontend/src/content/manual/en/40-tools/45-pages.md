# Pages

Turns your material into a learning page with pictures, tables and charts. You can then edit the
page and share it with students.

## Creating a page

You choose material and instruction as in Report: up to 10 files or sources from a workspace,
plus an optional instruction such as “only chapter 4” or “for first-year students”. The page is
made in the background, its card under Your pages shows the progress.

The system decides how each section is shown, by its content: terms as cards, a procedure as
steps, measured values as a chart, comparisons as a table. On top come a summary, a mind map of
all sections, a word cloud of the technical terms and one picture at the top that stands for
the topic of the whole page. In the guide look the picture is a section of its own above the
title box, you move, hide or delete it like any section. Figures always come
from the material. A page holds no quiz questions, it only condenses.

Under the title the page only says that it was made with AI. What it was made from and with is
behind the i icon on its card: material, workspace, instruction and AI provider. Only you see it.

## Editing

Top right you switch between Edit and Preview.

- Click into any text to change it. Enter finishes the input.
- Every section has the buttons Move up, Move down, Hide and Delete, the title block too. Hidden
  sections are seen by nobody but you.
- Hover over an element and a bin appears that deletes exactly this element.
- Undo and Redo go back 20 steps, also with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z.
- Only Save keeps your changes. Reset brings back the last saved version.

The plus between two sections inserts a new one. You choose a layout with one to four fields
and decide for each field what goes in: text, image, table, chart, list or note. Nobody sees an
empty field when reading. Under lists and tables, Point, Row and Column add further entries.

If the top picture is missing, Add image puts one in, the AI suggests a description of the
topic for it. Every image can be replaced with Recreate image. The description is in English, a new image
also gets the page's image style. The old image stays until the new one is ready, and Undo
brings it back afterwards too. At most 40 images can be created per page. Charts, the mind map
and the word cloud are changed with Edit data: labels, values and terms.

So that a page never breaks, there are limits: headings and short texts up to 120 characters,
list items and card texts up to 300, longer texts up to 1,500, at most 20 sections.

To keep a page as a PDF, use the browser's print dialog (Ctrl+P). The editing buttons do not
appear there.

## Sharing

Every page starts private. The switch on the card or the share icon in the editor makes it
shareable, you get a link. Only people with a KIS account can open it, they see the preview and
cannot change anything. Switch back to private and the link stops working. Switch on again and
the same link works again.

Your collection shows every page you currently share under one fixed link. Newly shared pages
appear there by themselves.


## Languages

Every page comes in German and in English. German material gives a German page and right after
it the English version, material in any other language the other way round. The second version
takes one to two minutes longer, the languages icon in the editor shows how far it is. Readers
switch at the top right, and the link remembers the language.

You edit the second version like the original. If you change the original afterwards, it
shows as older than the page in the window. Update carries it over again and overwrites changes
you made in this language. The learning assistant in a workspace reads only the original.

## In a workspace

You can put a finished page into one of your workspaces as material: in the workspace, use the
plus at Materials and pick it under Page from Pages. Or copy the link of the shared page and read
it in there under Read in a web page. The learning assistant reads the page section
by section, answers from it and cites it by section number. A click on the material or on a
citation opens the page in a new tab, at the cited section. Every member of the workspace can
read it there, even when it is not shared. When you save a change to the page, the workspace
reads it again by itself.

## Keeping pages

Pages do not count towards the pool of your jobs and are never pushed out by newer jobs. You can
keep up to 50 pages. Delete a page and its link is gone too.
