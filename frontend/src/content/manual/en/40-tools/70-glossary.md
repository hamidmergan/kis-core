# Glossary

Here you keep your own term lists, so technical terms are translated the same way everywhere.

![The glossary service](/manual/{lang}/tool-glossary.png)

## Creating a glossary

A new one starts with **+ New glossary**. An entry consists of the German and the English term.

## Where it takes effect

- **Translation** and **Interpreter** translate the terms the way you decided, instead of the
  way the machine thinks is right.
- **Screencast**: chosen in the Upload step, it applies to the translation of the slides and to
  the scripts.

Everywhere up to 3 glossaries can be chosen at once. If a term is in several, the glossary at
the top of the selection wins.

## Import and export

A glossary can be exported as JSON and read back in. That way you pass a well kept list on to
colleagues.

## Limits

At most 10 glossaries per person and 500 entries per glossary. Every glossary shows next to its
name how many entries it has. Once a limit is reached, the button says Maximum reached with the
count.

## Do not mix them up

This glossary serves the translation. The glossary in the studio of a workspace is something else:
it collects the technical terms of the workspace, and flashcards and vocab cards can be
generated from it.
