# Translation

Translates texts and documents between German and English. Layout and formatting are kept, so a
presentation comes back as a presentation.

![The translation service](/manual/{lang}/tool-translation.png)

## Two ways in

- **Enter text**: up to 100,000 characters, the counter below the field keeps count. The
  translation appears on the page and can be copied with one click. If you change the text
  afterwards, the page says that the result no longer matches.
- **Upload file**: Word, PowerPoint, Markdown, LaTeX or text, that is .docx, .pptx, .md, .tex and
  .txt. PDF does not work. A file may be 500 MB in size, its text at most 100,000 characters. The
  result comes as a file in the same format.

## Languages and provider

Currently only German and English. The source language is detected automatically, you choose the
target language. If there are several AI providers, you choose that too.

## Fixing technical terms

Up to 3 glossaries from the glossary service take effect at the same time. If a term is in
several, the glossary at the top of the selection wins.

## Duration

Large documents take a few minutes. The job keeps running in the background, the result waits
under Jobs.
