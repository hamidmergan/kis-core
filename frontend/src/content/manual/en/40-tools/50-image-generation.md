# Image generation

Three tasks in one workplace, switched at the top with the tabs Create image, Edit image and
Describe image. The form is on the left, the image on the right, and below it, under Your images,
everything you have created or uploaded so far.

![The image generation service](/manual/{lang}/tool-image.png)

## Everything is connected

A click on an image under Your images shows it large. The tabs at the top then edit or describe it
right away, the buttons below it download and delete it. A result thus becomes the source for the next step, without a
detour over the hard disk. Your own images come in as PNG or JPG up to 20 MB, dragging works too.

## Settings at the image

A collapsible band at the image holds the settings:

- **Style**: No style, Photo, Illustration, Sketch, Watercolor, 3D, Flat or THL slide style
- **Format**: Square 1:1, Slide 16:9, Landscape 4:3, Portrait 3:4, Portrait 9:16 or Like source
- **Size** and **Quality**: higher quality takes longer
- **Variants**: up to 4 when creating, up to 2 when editing. Every variant is a job of its own.
  If you already have other jobs open, the page starts fewer and says so.
- **No lettering in the image**: set by default, because image models often misspell words
- **Expand prompt**: the AI expands your description into a detailed prompt. You see it, can
  change it, and exactly that goes to the image model.

If there are several AI providers, you choose one per task.

## Editing

The source stays next to the result, before and after can be compared directly. Quick actions
save the typing: White background, As a sketch, Remove text, Stronger colors and THL slide style.

## Describing

Three tasks: Describe, Read text or Own question, with the answer in German or English. After the
first answer you ask further questions about the same image. The answer can be copied.
