# What the language trainer is

![The three tools of the language trainer in the studio](/manual/{lang}/studio.png)

For everybody still learning the language of the course. Three tools in the studio: vocabulary
cards, vocabulary exercises, grammar exercises. All built from the materials of the workspace,
matched to your own level.

## What the level does

- The assistant in a workspace answers at that level
- Everything generated is worded to match: flashcards, quizzes, vocabulary cards, vocabulary and
  grammar exercises, mind map, data table, glossary
- Content, the number of items and the solutions stay the same, only wording and depth change
- Without a level none of this happens

## Accepted once, applies everywhere

The language trainer hangs on you, not on the workspace. The start page carries a card for it next
to the demo workspace. Start the language test accepts it, from then on it applies in every workspace. A switch to turn it on or off later does not exist yet.

Without a level it stays closed. Only with a level, from the language test or set yourself under
Language level, do the three tools appear in the studio.

## What the administration decides

Only whether the platform offers the language trainer at all. If it is off there, the card is
missing from the start page and nobody sees it.
