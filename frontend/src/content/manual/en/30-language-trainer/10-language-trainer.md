# What the language trainer is

![The three tools of the language trainer in the studio](/manual/{lang}/studio.png)

For German as a foreign language. Three tools in the studio: Vocab cards, Vocabulary exercises,
Grammar exercises. All built from the materials of the workspace, matched to your own level.

## In which language

The language test is always in German. Vocab cards and vocabulary exercises currently are too,
whatever language the materials are in. In grammar exercises the task line is German, the
sentences themselves are written by the AI in the language of the material. The administration's
language setting for flashcards and quiz does not apply here.

## What the level does

- The chat in a workspace answers at that level
- Everything generated is worded to match: flashcards, quizzes, vocab cards, vocabulary and
  grammar exercises, mind map, data table, glossary
- Content, the number of items and the solutions stay the same, only wording and depth of
  explanation change
- Without a level none of this happens

## Accepted once, applies everywhere

The language trainer belongs to the person, not to the workspace. Above the workspace list there
is a card for it, in the row with the tour and the evaluation. Start the language test switches it
on and leads into the test, from then on it applies in every workspace. Hide switches it off.

The card only stays until the first answer. After that the switch Use the language trainer under
Language level turns it on and off.

Without a level it stays closed. Only with a level, from the language test or set yourself under
Language level, do the three tools appear in the studio.

## What the administration decides

- **The platform switch** under Learning assistant, Language level. It is off by default, then the card
  is missing and nobody sees the language trainer. Levels and choices already made stay stored
  and apply again once it is on
- **The permission Own language level and language test**: none of the system roles holds it,
  administrators included. The administration creates a custom role for it and assigns it.
  Without the permission the card is missing, the Language level page says the language trainer
  is not enabled for the account, and the three tools are missing from the studio
