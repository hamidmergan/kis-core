# Vocabulary exercises

A vocabulary exercise is a text from the materials with the technical terms missing. You fill
the missing words in.

![The vocabulary exercises in the module](/manual/{lang}/vocabulary-exercise-sets.png)

## Two ways to an exercise

While creating it you choose the basis:

- **From selected materials**: the terms come straight out of the ticked materials
- **From a set of vocabulary cards**: the terms of the cards become the gaps

The second way pays off when you already worked through a set of vocabulary cards and now want
to use the same words in a sentence. For that the set has to be finished beforehand.

On top of that you decide how many gaps the text should have.

![A vocabulary exercise](/manual/{lang}/vocabulary-exercise.png)

## While solving

You type a word into every gap and check. Afterwards it says how many gaps were right. An
example can be unfolded for every gap.

## What it is for

A term you put into a real sentence from your own lecture sits differently than one you only
read on a card.
