# Grammar exercises

A grammar exercise practises a grammar topic on new sentences. The AI writes them from the facts
in the ticked materials, in the language of the material. So the content comes from the material,
not the wording. Citations on the exercises lead to the matching passage.

![The grammar exercises in the workspace](/manual/{lang}/grammar-sets.png)

## Four exercise formats

When creating an exercise you choose a topic, 5, 10 or 15 sentences and at least one of four
formats. Several formats share the sentences roughly evenly. No instruction can be added here.

- **Rewrite**: write the whole sentence anew following the pattern of the topic, for example from
  active to passive
- **Fill the gap**: type the missing form. The base form stands in brackets after the gap
- **True or false**: decide whether a sentence uses the practised form correctly. About half of
  these sentences contain a mistake in exactly that form
- **Multiple choice**: pick the correct one out of three complete sentences. The other two are
  rewrites of the same sentence with one typical mistake each

## Before the exercise appears

KIS has the AI check every finished sentence once more. Sentences that turn ungrammatical with the
solution or do not show the practised form at all are dropped. That is why an exercise sometimes
has fewer sentences than chosen. If fewer than half are left, it fails.

![A grammar exercise](/manual/{lang}/grammar-exercise.png)

## While solving

- **Typed formats**: KIS checks Rewrite and Fill the gap without an AI call, by comparing with the
  expected solution and the further correct versions the AI supplies when generating.
  Capitalisation and punctuation do not count. The verdict has three steps: Correct, Almost, Not
  right yet. Almost means at least 72 % of the words match a correct version
- **Clicked formats**: True or false and Multiple choice are decided with the click, here there
  is only correct or incorrect
- **After answering**: The expected solution only appears when the answer was not correct. It
  comes with a short AI explanation of which form decides the case
- **Example**: With Rewrite, the first pattern pair of the topic can be unfolded below the input
  field
- **Back and Next**: always work. An exercise can be skipped and solved later, an answered one
  stays locked

A version that is just as correct but was not supplied by the AI counts as Almost or as a mistake.
At the end it says how many sentences were correct. Retry wrong currently starts the whole exercise
again.

## The topics

Every new workspace comes with eight topics: Passiv Präsens, Passiv mit Modalverb, Perfekt,
Präteritum, Konjunktiv II, Relativsätze, Nebensätze and Adjektivdeklination. Any member can add
more through New topic in the create window.

- **From the course**: topics by the owner and tutors, plus the eight the workspace came with.
  Everyone in the workspace sees them
- **Your own topics**: If someone who is neither owner nor tutor creates a topic, only that
  person sees it. Owner and tutors see one shared list without this split
- **Deleting**: Everyone deletes their own topics, owner and tutors any topic. Finished exercises
  on a deleted topic are kept

Owner and tutors, too, only reach the window through the Grammar exercises tile, so only with the
language trainer switched on and a level set.

## Creating a topic

- **Name**: free, such as "Konjunktiv II". Check has the AI build a pattern pair from it, a
  sentence before and the same sentence after. It also writes a rule that is not shown but steers
  the exercises
- **Not a grammar topic**: If the AI finds no grammar topic in the name, KIS creates nothing
- **Understood as**: If the AI turns the name into a more precise form, it suggests that name. Use
  this name puts it in
- **Pattern pairs**: The suggested pair can be overwritten. + Another pattern adds pairs up to
  three, useful for topics with several correct constructions such as Modalsätze with "indem" and
  "dadurch, dass"
- **Accept** saves the topic. After that it can no longer be changed, only deleted

Check and Generate again each count as one generation against the quota.
