# Grammar exercises

A grammar exercise takes sentences from the ticked materials and has them reshaped after a
pattern, for example from active into passive.

![The grammar exercises in the module](/manual/{lang}/grammar-sets.png)

## Four task formats

While creating it you choose the topic, the number of sentences and at least one of four
formats.

- **Transform**: rewrite the sentence following the instruction
- **Fill the gap**: enter the missing form
- **True or false**: decide about a statement
- **Multiple choice**: pick the fitting form out of several

![A grammar exercise](/manual/{lang}/grammar-exercise.png)

## While solving

You write your answer and have it checked. The verdict has three steps: right, close, not right
yet. Every sentence comes with a model solution.

Close means the direction is correct but something else is off, for example a case or an ending.

## Which topics exist

The topics sit in a catalogue kept by the administration. Among others you can choose present
passive, passive with a modal verb, perfect, simple past, subjunctive II, relative clauses,
subordinate clauses and adjective declension.

## What it can fail on

The topics come from the catalogue, the sentences from your materials. If the materials say
nothing about a grammatical form, the exercise cannot build anything about it. That is not a
fault but the rule that everything comes from the sources.
