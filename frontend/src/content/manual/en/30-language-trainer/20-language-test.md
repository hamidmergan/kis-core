# The language test

The language test estimates how well you already know the language. The result is a level from
A1 to C2.

![A question in the language test](/manual/{lang}/language-test.png)

## When it comes

The start page carries the card Language trainer next to the demo workspace, for students and for
teaching staff. Start the language test begins the test at once. Whoever picks Hide has the
language trainer off and currently cannot switch it back on alone yet.

## How it runs

The AI asks a few questions one after another. Two kinds appear: questions to click and
questions you answer freely. You may skip single questions. The top always says which question
you are on.

At the end your level stands there with a short reasoning.

## In which language

The test is in German. Questions and grading too, this cannot be changed.

## What the result does

The level applies in every workspace. It steers how hard the exercises in the language trainer
turn out and how thoroughly the chat explains. Under Language level you can retake the test or set
the grade yourself through Change level. It is not an examination.

## How reliable it is

The result comes from an AI, which can make mistakes. It is a placement for the exercises, not a
certificate. Whoever rates themselves differently sets the grade under Language level.
