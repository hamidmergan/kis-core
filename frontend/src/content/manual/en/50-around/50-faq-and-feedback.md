# FAQ and feedback

Three ways to look something up quickly, report something, or say how KIS is working out for you.

## FAQ

The FAQ in the gear menu, right above the documentation, is visible to every signed-in account
without any permission. It is sorted by problem, the documentation by feature, in four groups:
Basics, Data and law, The AI assistant Francis, Day to day. If a question is missing, the box
Question not covered? leads to the documentation and to the feedback form.

![The FAQ page with one question expanded](/manual/{lang}/faq.png)

## Report a bug or share an idea

The link sits in the footer of every page, inside a workspace in its settings.

![The Report a bug or share an idea form](/manual/{lang}/feedback-form.png)

- **Anonymous**: The entry is stored without any link to your account. Only with the checkbox
  Show my username or an entered contact address can it be traced to you
- **Category**: Bug, Idea or Other
- **Message**: up to 2,000 characters. For a bug it helps to say what you did and what happened,
  that saves a follow-up question
- **Attachments**: up to 10 files, PNG, JPG or PDF, up to 15 MB each
- **Page**: KIS notes from which page you opened the form
- **Who reads it**: the administration under Users on the Feedback page, with the permission View
  anonymous feedback

## Evaluation

A survey by the administration that you fill in from the user's point of view.

![A scale question of the evaluation](/manual/{lang}/evaluation-question.png)

- **When**: only while the administration has an evaluation switched on. It applies either to
  every account or only to accounts from an access code the evaluation is attached to, plus
  everyone allowed to manage the evaluation
- **Way in**: through the card Quick evaluation on the start page or at any time through
  Evaluation in the gear menu
- **Flow**: question by question, scales from 1 to 5 and free text fields. On a scale it moves on
  by itself after the click, Back returns to a question. Without a single answer nothing is sent
- **No names**: The results show the answers without the account
- **More than once**: After submitting, the card disappears. Through the gear you can still take
  part again, every submission counts on its own
