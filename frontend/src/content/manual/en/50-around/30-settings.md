# Settings

![The menu behind the gear](/manual/{lang}/header-menu.png)

The gear in the top right applies to the whole platform. Everyone sees Language and Appearance,
the other entries depend on permissions and on what the administration has switched on:

- **Language** of the interface, German or English. KIS remembers it on your account, for the next
  time as well
- **Appearance**, light or dark. The browser remembers that
- **Language level**, with the permission Own language level and language test
- **Interfaces**, connect your own voice services, with the permission Set up own interfaces to
  voice services. Teaching staff have it from the start
- **Tour through the demo workspace**, as long as the administration provides a demo workspace
- **Evaluation**, as long as an evaluation is running. The way back once the card on the start
  page is gone
- **Documentation**, these pages, with the permission View the manual
- **Admin**, **Administration** or **Statistics**, depending on the role's administrative
  permissions. Whoever holds all of them sees Admin, whoever may only read numbers sees
  Statistics, everyone else Administration
- **Sign out**
