# Interfaces

Under Interfaces in the gear menu you connect your own account at a voice service. Its voices are
then available to you in Text to audio, Podcast and Screencast.

![The Interfaces page with three connected providers](/manual/{lang}/interfaces.png)

## Currently voices only

At the moment you can connect ElevenLabs, Gemini and Fish Audio. Your own language models for
chat, translation or summaries cannot be connected here, KIS keeps using the university's models
for those.

## A notice first

On your first visit the page stays locked until you confirm a notice. Its main points:

- **Your contract**: You use your account under the provider's terms. Costs and the rights to
  the results are yours.
- **Your texts leave**: Whatever you have spoken goes to the provider and so leaves the systems
  of TH Lübeck.
- **Cloned voices**: only your own or that of a person who has agreed.

If the notice changes in substance, KIS asks you again.

## Entering the key

![A card before connecting, with a key entered](/manual/{lang}/interfaces-key.png)

When you connect, KIS checks the key with the provider right away and fetches the voices along the
way. If the provider rejects the key, you see it on this card at once and not only with your first
job.

## Which voices appear

- **ElevenLabs**: every voice in your account, including the ones you cloned yourself
- **Gemini**: 30 fixed voices, each of them speaks every language
- **Fish Audio**: your own voices plus the 20 best rated public German and 10 English ones

If you create a new voice at the provider, Reload voices brings it into KIS. In the voice picker
the provider is marked as own account.

## Only for you

- **Visibility**: Nobody but you sees your voices, neither other teaching staff nor students.
- **Key**: KIS stores it encrypted and afterwards shows only its last four characters. It is used
  for your own jobs only.
- **Balance**: KIS shows it only for ElevenLabs, and only if the key may read your plan. For
  Gemini and Fish Audio you see it in your account at the provider.

## When the provider does not play along

If the provider rejects the key or your balance is used up, the job stops with a message that
names the provider. If it only fails to answer for a moment, KIS tries again.

KIS never falls back to the university's voices here, otherwise a podcast would switch to another
voice halfway through.

## Disconnecting

Remove deletes the key in KIS and the voices disappear from the picker. At the provider the key
stays valid until you delete it there.
