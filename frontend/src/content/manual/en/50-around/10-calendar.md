# Calendar

The calendar in the header collects the dates from all your workspaces in one place.

![The calendar](/manual/{lang}/calendar.png)

## What is in it

All dates from the schedules of your workspaces, plus your own entries. Where a date comes from is
stated on the card, clicking it opens the workspace's schedule. The other way round, the calendar
icon in a schedule leads here.

## Using it

You page from month to month and get back with **Today**. Clicking a day shows what is due on
that day, a second click shortly after creates your own entry there. If nothing is due, the
calendar says so for the day and not for the month.

## Done

Every date can be ticked off. That is a private marking, others do not see it. Done dates stay,
struck through.

## Hiding

The eye on a date only takes it out of the month grid, it stays in the list beside it. Only this
browser remembers that, on another device the date is back.

## Without a fixed day

If the material names only a month, the date sits in no cell but under **No fixed day**. If the
source gives no year, KIS takes the year that puts the date closest to today and marks that with a
small question mark at the year.

## Your own dates

**Add entry** creates one of your own, also spanning several days and with a time. Your own
entries belong to you alone and appear in no workspace. Only they can be edited and deleted here,
the dates from a workspace are maintained in its schedule.
