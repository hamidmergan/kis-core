# Calendar

The calendar in the header collects the dates from all your modules in one place.

![The calendar](/manual/{lang}/calendar.png)

## What is in it

All dates from the schedules of your modules, plus your own entries. Where a date comes from is
stated on the card.

## Using it

You page from month to month and get back with **Today**. Clicking a day shows what is due on
that day. If nothing is due, the calendar says so for the day and not for the month.

## Done

Every date can be ticked off. That is a private marking, others do not see it.

## Without a fixed day

Dates that stand in the material without a date do not vanish. They gather under **Without a
fixed day**.

## Your own dates

**Add date** creates one of your own. These dates are private.
