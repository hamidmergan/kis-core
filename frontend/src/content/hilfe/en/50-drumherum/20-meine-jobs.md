# My jobs

Every order given to a tool is a job. The job list shows what is running, what is finished and
what went wrong.

![The job list](/hilfe/{lang}/meine-jobs.png)

## Where it is

Through **Jobs** in the header. A red dot next to it means something needs your attention.

## What you do there

Filter by tool, period or file name. Download finished results. Repeat failed jobs.

## States

- **Done**: the result is ready
- **Waiting**: the job sits in the queue
- **Error**: something went wrong, repeating usually helps
- **Expired**: the result was removed after a while

## The list is limited

The last 100 jobs are kept. When a new one arrives, the oldest drops out. So download results
you want to keep in good time.
