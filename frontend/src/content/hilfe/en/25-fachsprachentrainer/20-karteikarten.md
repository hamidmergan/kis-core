# Flashcards

![A flashcard](/hilfe/{lang}/uebung-karteikarten.png)

Cards with a question and an answer from the materials. You read the question, reveal the answer
and say whether you knew it.

At the end it says how many you knew and how many stayed open.

## Do not mix them up with vocabulary cards

Flashcards ask about the **content**. The **vocabulary cards** in the language trainer explain a
technical word linguistically.
