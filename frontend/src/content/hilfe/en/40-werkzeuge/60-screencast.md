# Screencast

Turns a PowerPoint file into a narrated video, in another language if you want.

![The screencast tool](/hilfe/{lang}/werkzeug-screencast.png)

## The steps

1. Upload a .pptx file
2. Decide whether the slides should be translated, and into which language
3. Choose the language of the spoken texts
4. Look through the suggested spoken text per slide and change it
5. Have it narrated and download the video

The source language is detected automatically.

## The spoken texts are yours

The AI suggests a text for every slide. You can overwrite each of them before the narration
starts. That is the step where the quality is decided.

## Subtitles

The settings let you switch on subtitles for the video.

## How long it takes

By far the largest part of the waiting time goes into the narration. With many slides that runs
accordingly long, but the job keeps running in the background.

At most five jobs sit in the queue at the same time. If there are more, wait or delete one in
the job list.
