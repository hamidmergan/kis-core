# The interface

![The start page with your own modules](/hilfe/{lang}/startseite.png)

The start page has three areas.

- **On top** the logo (always leads back here), on the right calendar, jobs and the gear
- **On the left** the tools, but only those released for you
- **In the middle** your modules with the number of materials and their state

## The gear

![The menu behind the gear](/hilfe/{lang}/kopfleiste.png)

Language, light or dark look, this help, sign out. Administrators also find the administration
area here.

## Active and inactive

The switch behind each module locks it for participants. Nothing is deleted, one click releases
it again.
