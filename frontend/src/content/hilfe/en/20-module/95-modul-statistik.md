# Statistics inside the module

The Statistics tab shows how far the participants got with the exercises. How many sets were
worked through, how much of it was right, where it gets stuck.

![The Statistics tab in the studio](/hilfe/{lang}/modul-statistik.png)

## What it is good for

For teachers it is the quickest look at whether the exercises land and which topics cause
trouble. For participants it is their own state.

## What it is not

Not a grade and not an assessment. The numbers say what was used, not how good somebody is.
Whoever runs an exercise three times sees different numbers than somebody who solves it once
correctly.
