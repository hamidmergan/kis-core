# Image generation

Three things in one tool, switched with the tabs on top.

![The image generation tool](/doku/{lang}/werkzeug-bild.png)

- **Text to image**: you describe a picture and it is created
- **Edit image**: you upload a picture and describe the change you want
- **Analyse image**: you upload a picture and get a description

## While creating

Describe the picture and choose the size. If the result does not fit, **regenerate** helps, or a
more precise description.

## While analysing

You can ask a specific question about the picture. Leave the field empty and the picture is
described completely. That is also the quickest way to an alternative text for accessibility.

## What may be uploaded

PNG or JPG, at most 20 MB.
