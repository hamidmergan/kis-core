# What the subject language trainer is

![The tiles in the studio](/doku/{lang}/modul-studio.png)

Four tiles in the Tools tab of the studio. Each builds something different out of the ticked
materials: flashcards, quiz, data table, mind map.

Unlike the language trainer this is about the **content** of the subject, not about the
language. It is always there, no release needed.

## How a set comes about

Tick materials, click the tile, choose the number, create.

![The window while creating](/doku/{lang}/modul-erstellen.png)

The creation runs in the background. Finished sets then sit below the tile and in the recent
activities.

## Two limits

- **At most five materials per creation**, even when more are ticked. A hint above the button
  says so
- **A stock per tile**, the button shows the state, for example 3 of 20. Deleting old sets makes
  room
